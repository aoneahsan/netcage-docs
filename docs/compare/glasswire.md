GlassWire suits the question of which app is eating your data plan, with blocking as the second step. NetCage starts after that question: you already know which app should be offline, and you cage it with one switch, without a usage dashboard in the way.

That’s on paper for now, because NetCage can’t be installed yet: no listing, no public download.

## What happens to your data graphs?

They go. GlassWire leads with graphs and data-limit alerts, and blocking comes after. NetCage’s optional data totals come from Android’s own accounting, per user ID, and they aren’t a usage dashboard.

GlassWire’s firewall is built on the same VpnService that NetCage uses, so only one of them can be on at a time.

## What should a GlassWire user read?

Nothing imports from GlassWire. Once you know the apps you’d cage, these pages pick up from there:

- [Caging apps](../caging-apps.md): the switch, and where the data totals appear.
