NetCage can’t be installed today. It has no store listing and no public download, so none of these pages ends in an install step. If you need something on your phone now, every app below is there to get and NetCage isn’t.

Each page says how that app differs from NetCage, who it suits better and which pages here to read first. Facts about the other app come from its own pages, with the link and the date. Where its pages don’t say, the row reads “Not stated on the pages we checked”.

## Which app are you coming from?

| App | What it is | Checked |
|---|---|---|
| [NetGuard](./netguard.md) | A no-root Android firewall that allows or denies internet per app on Wi-Fi and mobile, with paid filtering by address. | 7 November 2026 |
| [Rethink: DNS + Firewall + VPN](./rethinkdns.md) | An open-source Android app that combines a DNS resolver, a per-app firewall and WireGuard VPN connections. | 7 November 2026 |
| [AFWall+](./afwall-plus.md) | A front end for the iptables firewall on rooted Android phones, choosing which apps may use Wi-Fi, mobile data, roaming, LAN and VPN. | 7 November 2026 |
| [InviZible Pro](./invizible-pro.md) | An Android privacy app bundling Tor, DNSCrypt and I2P, with a firewall that allows or blocks internet per app. | 7 November 2026 |
| [TrackerControl](./trackercontrol.md) | A research project’s Android app that shows which tracking companies your apps contact and blocks trackers through a local VPN. | 7 November 2026 |
| [Blokada](./blokada.md) | An ad and tracker blocker for Android and iOS, with a free open-source app and subscription editions for cloud blocking, family controls and VPN. | 7 November 2026 |
| [Karma Firewall](./karma-firewall.md) | A small open-source Android firewall on F-Droid: pick the apps whose traffic you want blocked, with no IP or port rules to learn. | 7 November 2026 |
| [GlassWire Data Usage Monitor](./glasswire.md) | A data usage monitor for Android with graphs, data-limit alerts and a VpnService-based firewall for blocking apps. | 7 November 2026 |
| [AdGuard Ad Blocker for Android](./adguard-android.md) | An ad blocker for Android that filters ads and trackers in browsers and apps through a local VPN, sold as a yearly or lifetime licence. | 7 November 2026 |
| [PCAPdroid](./pcapdroid.md) | An open-source network monitor for Android that captures and analyses app traffic without root, with a paid firewall add-on. | 7 November 2026 |
| [Firewall No Root: App Firewall](./firewall-no-root.md) | A no-root Android firewall from Germany that allows or blocks internet per app, separately for Wi-Fi and cellular, with optional host rules and a DNS changer. | 7 November 2026 |

## What should I know about NetCage first?

It does one thing: it refuses a chosen app’s connections on this phone and leaves other apps alone. It isn’t an ad blocker or a content filter. [Limitations](../limitations.md) lists what that costs you, and [How it works](../how-it-works.md) explains the tunnel.

The same comparison is on [the NetCage website](https://netcage.aoneahsan.com/compare).
