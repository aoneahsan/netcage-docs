Firewall No Root is the choice if you want host and domain rules, filter lists and a DNS changer beside the per-app switch, and it’s on Google Play today. NetCage is the switch and little else to manage: no lists to update and no DNS to change. When a friend wants your setup, you share a Cage Pack.

NetCage for Android is ready to install from its website. [Download the signed APK](https://netcage.aoneahsan.com/download), then check its SHA-256 against the value on the home page before opening it.

## What’s different beyond the switch?

The extras point in different directions. Both give you a per-app switch on Wi-Fi and cellular without root. Firewall No Root adds host and domain rules, filter lists and a DNS changer, none of which NetCage has. NetCage adds profiles, schedules with day passes, and Cage Packs.

If Firewall No Root holds Android’s VPN slot, NetCage can’t hold it as well; there is only one.

## What should you read first?

Your host rules have nowhere to go; NetCage imports nothing from Firewall No Root. These pages show what it offers in their place:

- [Caging apps](../caging-apps.md): the switch, and rules for Wi-Fi and mobile.
- [Schedules and day passes](../schedules-and-day-passes.md): what NetCage does in place of host rules.
- [Cage Packs](../cage-packs.md): passing your setup to someone else.
