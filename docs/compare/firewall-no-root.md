## How does Firewall No Root differ from NetCage?

Both give you a per-app switch on Wi-Fi and cellular without root. Firewall No Root adds host and domain rules, filter lists and a DNS changer. NetCage has none of those. What it has instead is profiles, schedules with day passes, and shareable Cage Packs.

Android allows one VPN at a time. If Firewall No Root holds that slot on your phone, NetCage can’t hold it as well.

Choose Firewall No Root if you want host and domain rules, filter lists and a DNS changer beside the per-app switch, and it is on Google Play today.

Choose NetCage if you want the switch and nothing else to manage: no lists to update, no DNS to change, and shareable Cage Packs when a friend wants your setup.

## What should I read first?

NetCage imports nothing from Firewall No Root, so there’s no settings file to bring across. You’d choose your apps again.

- [Caging apps](../caging-apps.md): the switch, and rules for Wi-Fi and mobile.
- [Schedules and day passes](../schedules-and-day-passes.md): what NetCage does in place of host rules.
- [Cage Packs](../cage-packs.md): passing your setup to someone else.
