A business can offer NetCage to its own customers under its own name. We build the Android app with that
business's name, icon and colours, sign it, publish it and run the online services it signs in to. The
business sells it; we keep its updates coming.

Prices are not repeated in these docs. The [white-label pricing page](https://netcage.aoneahsan.com/white-label/pricing)
reads them live from our licensing service.

## Two ways to buy

| | White-label licence | Source buyout |
|---|---|---|
| Who runs the app | We do, under your name | You do |
| Source code | Stays with us | Handed over |
| Can NetCage be sold to others | Yes, until someone buys it outright | No, never again |
| Updates included | Six months | Six months |
| Ongoing payment | Renewals, if you want more updates | A 7% royalty on the gross revenue NetCage earns, for as long as it earns |

A buyout is described on the [buyout page](https://netcage.aoneahsan.com/white-label/buyout). Licences sold
before a buyout keep their terms. The buyout royalty is 7% of the gross revenue NetCage earns, not counting
sales tax or VAT collected, paid every quarter with a revenue statement for as long as NetCage earns revenue.
It is still owed after the buyer sells NetCage on; the
[licence terms](https://netcage.aoneahsan.com/white-label/license-terms) set it out.

Licences, renewals and buyouts are paid by bank transfer to our Wise account. The licence is issued once we
have confirmed the transfer.

## What NetCage ships

NetCage is an Android app, and only an Android app. A licence therefore covers one platform and there is no
bundle discount to apply. There is no iPhone version today; if a buyer wants one, it is built on request once
the buyer provides or funds an Apple developer account.

## Changes made only for you

A licence covers NetCage as it ships. A change for one buyer's copy, or a product of the buyer's own, is
custom work, quoted before anything starts. Ask on the [custom work page](https://netcage.aoneahsan.com/white-label/custom-work).
