A licence covers the platforms it was bought for. NetCage ships one: the Android app.

## The Android app

- The NetCage app for Android 8.0 and newer, with the buyer's name, icon and colours, on the buyer's own
  package name.
- A build signed for the buyer, published under the buyer's Google Play developer account, or offered as a
  signed APK download from the buyer's own site. NetCage itself ships the second way today.
- The online services the app signs in to: accounts, backup and restore, support replies and Cage Packs.
- The buyer's domain, connected for the app's links and its privacy policy.
- Six months of updates, rebuilt under the buyer's name.

Google reviews every app that uses Android's VPN slot. Whether Google approves a listing is Google's decision,
not ours.

## What a licence leaves out

- **Source code.** It changes hands only with a buyout.
- **Exclusivity.** Until NetCage is bought outright, it may be licensed to other buyers too.
- **An iPhone version.** None exists today. One is built on request once the buyer provides or funds an Apple
  developer account.
- **Third-party costs.** The buyer's domain, Google Play developer account and any paid service the buyer
  chooses are the buyer's.
