## Can I change the app myself?

Not with a licence, because the code stays with us. Ask for the change as custom work, or buy NetCage outright
and change whatever you like.

## What happens when the included updates end?

The copy keeps running on the version it has. A renewal starts the updates again.

## What happens after a buyout?

We hand the source over and stop selling NetCage. Licences sold before then keep their terms.

## Who pays for the domain and the developer account?

The buyer does. The domain, the Google Play developer account and any paid service the buyer chooses are the
buyer's.

## Can a licence be revoked?

Yes, if it is used outside its terms or on a domain it does not cover.
