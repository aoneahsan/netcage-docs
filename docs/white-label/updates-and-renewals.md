A licence or a buyout includes six months of updates from the day of payment. After that, the copy keeps
running on the version it has until the updates are renewed.

## What counts as an update

- New NetCage releases: fixes and new features, rebuilt under the buyer's name.
- The changes Google Play asks of every app each year, such as a newer target Android version.
- Shipping each update to the buyer's listing or download page.

A change made only for one buyer's copy is not an update. It is custom work, quoted on its own.

## Renewals

Renewals come in 3, 6 and 12 month lengths. A renewal adds its months to the end date the licence already has,
or to the renewal date if that end date has passed. Current prices are on the
[updates page](https://netcage.aoneahsan.com/white-label/updates), which also takes a renewal by licence key.

To see when a licence's updates end, [check the licence](./verify-a-licence.md).
