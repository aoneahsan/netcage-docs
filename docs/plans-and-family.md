NetCage's firewall is the same on every plan. A plan only changes the room your account has in the cloud and how
many questions Ask NetCage answers a day.

## Do I need a plan to cage apps?

No. Caging, profiles, schedules, day passes, the widget, the tile and the event log run on the phone, on every plan,
with or without an account.

## What do Plus and Family add?

More room. Plus keeps more backup versions and more custom icons and publishes any number of Cage Packs. It also
answers more Ask NetCage questions a day. Family gives each person on it its own room and the deepest answers. The
[pricing page](https://netcage.aoneahsan.com/pricing) shows every plan's current limits and price, and whether it is
on sale.

## How does Family work?

One account buys the seats, its own included, and invites the others by email. Each person signs in with their own
Google account and keeps their own cages; nobody on the plan sees anyone else's setup.

## How do I invite someone to Family?

On the phone, open **Settings ▸ Account ▸ Family**, enter their email address and tap **Send invite**; on the
website, use the Plan tab of your account. They accept while signed in with that address, and a pending invitation
can be cancelled from the same place.

## Can I earn Plus without paying?

Yes. Share your link from the account page. Friends who create an account with it earn you months of Plus; the
account page shows the rule and every month you've earned. A friend counts once their Google account confirms their
email.

## What happens when a plan ends?

Nothing on your phone changes. A cancelled plan runs to the end of the period you paid for; after that, icons and
packs over the Free limits stay, and new ones wait for room. Backup history keeps the size it reached, and each new
backup replaces the oldest.
