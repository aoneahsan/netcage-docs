NetCage tells you when a new version is out. Most updates arrive through Google Play by themselves; when one matters,
NetCage asks for it directly.

## Why does NetCage ask me to update?

A version has been replaced. For a normal update you'll see "NetCage has a new version, required when you next open
it." For an urgent one, NetCage shows "This version has to be updated" with the reason, and stays on that screen until
it's updated.

## What happens when I update?

**Update** opens Google Play's own update. Updating restarts NetCage. Without Auto-start on boot, switch the cage back
on afterwards.

## What if I'm offline?

NetCage keeps working. Nothing is blocked because a check couldn't reach the server; an update NetCage already told
you about still applies the next time you open it.

## Does the website update the same way?

Yes, and mostly without asking: an open tab moves to the new version on its next page change. If a version is
replaced, the site asks you to update; updating reloads the page.
