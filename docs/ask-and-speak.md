Two ways to get unstuck. Ask NetCage answers from the documentation, and Speak turns a spoken request into a
written one that a person on the NetCage team reads and answers.

## What can Ask NetCage answer?

Ask NetCage answers from the public documentation and links the pages it used. It gets only your question; your
phone, your apps and your account stay out of it. It lives on the Help screen in the app and on the website's Help
page.

## What if Ask NetCage can't answer?

The documentation doesn't cover that, so we won't guess. A person on the NetCage team can answer it. Write to us, or
speak the request.

## How many questions can I ask?

Each day has an allowance: a few without an account, more on each plan. The Help screen shows how many you have left.
They come back at midnight, your time.

## What is Speak?

Say what happened, or what you'd like NetCage to do. We'll write it up as a request you can read and change before
anything is sent. Speak runs on the website: the Speak button at the top of every page, or Speak a request on Help.

## What happens to the recording?

Where your browser can, it types your words as you talk. The recording goes to NetCage's server only to be written
up, with no app list or phone data, and isn't kept there; a copy stays in this browser until you send or close the
request. It stops by itself at 2:00.

## Where does the reply go?

Signed in, the reply comes back to your account, under Support, and isn't emailed. Signed out, it comes by email to
the address you give.
